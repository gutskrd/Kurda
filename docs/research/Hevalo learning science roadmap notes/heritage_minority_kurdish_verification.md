# Fact-check: heritage_minority_kurdish.md (heritage, minority-language and Kurdish context)

**How this check was done. Read this first.**
- **I could not run any web searches.** My first three searches returned "Web search was not performed: this turn's web search budget is used up (limit: 200 WebSearch calls per turn, shared by every agent in it)". WebFetch is blocked by the egress proxy. I did not try to get around either limit, for example with curl or a reader or proxy service.
- I used four other routes instead:
  1. **The Hevalo repository itself.** Product claims about Hevalo were checked against code (`shared/src/locales.ts`, `api/content/`, `api/src/content/exercises.ts`, `shared/src/kurdish-text.ts`, `api/src/placement/placement.ts`, `docs/admin/dictionary-import.md`) and against the sibling `hevalo_current_state_*.md` notes. This is the only route that can produce a firm "confirmed" or "overstated" on product facts.
  2. **Independent retrievals by sibling researchers in this folder.** I counted a sibling note as corroboration only when it cites a *different* source URL or reports *different* details. Lines tagged "[SIB]", "via heritage_minority_kurdish.md" or "VIA SIBLING" are copies of this note, not corroboration. Sibling retrievals are also at snippet level.
  3. **Internal-consistency checks**, against the note's own evidence grades and its other sections.
  4. **My background knowledge.** This **never upgrades a claim to "confirmed"**. Where I use it, it is labelled "(background, unverified)" with no URL. I made up no URLs.
- **I did not open any URL in the Sources column.** Each one is either the original note's citation, a sibling's independent retrieval, or a repository path, and is labelled as such.
- **Verdict rules:**
  - **confirmed**: an independent retrieval or the repository matches the claim.
  - **overstated**: the core may hold, but a number, label, framing or design inference goes beyond the evidence, or independent evidence narrows it.
  - **refuted**: independent evidence contradicts the claim.
  - **unverified**: no independent corroboration was available this session. Following the task rules, I gave no benefit of the doubt, even when the claim matches my background knowledge.
- **Most "unverified" verdicts reflect the exhausted search budget, not a suspected error.** Re-run searches for rows 1, 4, 7 and 8 first, because downstream recommendations lean on them most.

## Omitted counter-evidence and caveats

1. **Hevalo's grader currently accepts the exact spelling errors a literacy track must catch.** `gradeText` and `checkWriting` in `api/src/content/exercises.ts` return `verdict: 'typo', accepted: true` when an answer matches only after folding ê→e, î→i, û→u, ş→s, ç→c. Listening transcriptions use the same rule. These letters are distinct phonemes that distinguish words, for example *se* "dog" vs *sê* "three" and *ser* "head" vs *şer* "fight" (examples are background, unverified). Rec 2's "diacritic-tolerant feedback" needs a separate **strict mode** for dictation and spelling items. Otherwise the literacy track counts its target errors as correct.
2. **Identity motivation predicts intended effort, not achievement.** Al-Hoorie's 2018 meta-analysis (independent retrieval in motivation_gamification.md) found that the ideal L2 self correlates r = .61 with *intended effort* but only r = .20 with achievement, about .10 after publication-bias correction. The note's strongest lever (identity and family, rec 5) therefore needs behavioural scaffolds such as scheduled, tracked practice. It will not raise outcomes on its own. — [ERIC EJ1202469](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1202469.pdf)
3. **Heritage speakers do worse on explicit, metalinguistic tests** (Bowles 2011, via sla_theory.md). Placement and grammar checkpoints built from rule questions will under-place exactly the learners rec 1 aims to fast-track. Use listening, oral and spelling probes instead. — [Montrul 2012](http://www.eurosla.org/Docs/Yearbook2012/Montrul.pdf)
4. **Heritage-language anxiety and dialect shaming.** Later-generation heritage speakers fear correction or mockery by "real" speakers (Sevinç & Dewaele 2018; cited from prior knowledge in social_collaborative.md, unverified). The community feed and "Ask your grandparent" missions (rec 5) can expose heritage learners to exactly this. They need moderation norms and a "no dialect shaming" policy, and should be private by default.
5. **Many young Kurds may be receptive-only or near-beginners, not "fluent speakers who can't read".** The note's own CASLT source says bans "stunted linguistic development in each new generation", and the note has no data on diaspora proficiency. The claim that segment 2 (in-region learners) is "often fluent speakers" is unquantified. A literacy-first track is right only for part of the audience. Placement must route weak speakers to an oral track as well.
6. **Streaks for children conflict with current EU and UK guidance.** The EU DSA Article 28 guidelines (July 2025) recommend that streaks and push notifications be **off by default for minors**. The UK Children's Code restricts reward-loop nudges (both independent retrievals in motivation_gamification.md). Rec 5's "family/household groups with shared streaks" should therefore be adult-only, or not loss-framed for child profiles. Hevalo's current streak reminders are loss-framed ("Don't lose your streak!") per hevalo_current_state_backend.md. — [European Commission DSA Art. 28 guidelines](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-guidelines-protection-minors)
7. **Legal requirements for family recordings and heritage questions are not in the note.**
   - **COPPA:** the 2025 amendments expanded personal information to include biometric identifiers, with a compliance deadline of 22 April 2026. Children's voice recordings are personal information.
   - **GDPR Article 8:** the age of digital consent is 13–16 depending on the country (children.md, independent retrieval). Hevalo has no consent flow today (hevalo_current_state_backend.md §11).
   - **Grandparents:** "Ask your grandparent" recordings need the grandparent's own consent, plus a licence if they feed ASR/TTS.
   - **GDPR Article 9:** onboarding questions about family heritage may reveal **ethnic origin**, a special category of data that needs explicit consent and minimisation (background, unverified). The risk is higher for users in Turkey, Iran and Syria.
   - Sources: [White & Case on the COPPA amendments (via children.md)](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/unpacking-ftcs-coppa-amendments-what-you-need-know); [EuConsent (via children.md)](https://euconsent.eu/digital-age-of-consent-under-the-gdpr/)
8. **Licensing limits on the proposed content partners.**
   - Zarok TV's best-known content is **dubbed third-party cartoons** (Smurfs, SpongeBob and others). A broadcaster's dubbing rights normally do not let it sublicense to an app (inference, unverified), so only Zarok's own productions are realistic. Zarok TV was also once closed by Turkish decree, which is a political exposure for a partner aimed at Turkey.
   - Meta MMS weights may be CC-BY-NC, which would bar commercial use (speech_audio_tech.md, flagged unverified).
   - The Wîkîferheng dictionary data is CC BY-SA, so any frequency-filtered dictionary derived from it (rec 10) must be shared alike (`docs/admin/dictionary-import.md`).
9. **The "beginner trap" is not unique to apps (background, unverified).** In Ireland, decades of compulsory schooling coexist with roughly 70k daily Irish speakers outside education (census). Wales's 2021 census showed fewer Welsh speakers than in 2011, despite millions of app learners. This supports the note's point that apps alone do not create speakers. It also cautions against assuming classroom tools (rec 11) solve it.
10. **LLM quality for Kurdish is untested and risky.** The note does not discuss LLMs. ai_llm_tutoring.md found (KurdEval, n = 5 Sorani prompts) that some open models inject Arabic or Turkish words and degrade badly. LLM-generated grammar notes on ergativity and ezafe are a known hallucination risk. Any AI-generated content in recs 3 and 7 needs native-speaker review. — [KurdEval (via ai_llm_tutoring.md)](https://github.com/Lavanmawlood/KurdEval)
11. **No outcome evidence exists anywhere in this domain.** The note says this clearly in its closing gap. It means every "should" in the Implications is a design hypothesis. Rec 14 (pre/post placement, delayed retention, A/B tests segmented by learner type) is the most defensible recommendation in the note, because it is the only way to turn these hypotheses into evidence.
