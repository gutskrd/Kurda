## 1. Leading and newest apps in 2025–2026: what they do, and which features have outcome evidence

### Takeaway
Duolingo is still the scale leader and the template: a linear path, XP and streaks, matched weekly leagues, a freemium limiter (hearts, moving to "energy" in 2025), and AI features (Max: Explain My Answer, Roleplay, Video Call with Lily). It had 58.7M DAU in Q2 2026, +23% YoY, after a 2025 "AI-first" pivot that caused public backlash, and its stated 2026 priority is "teaching better" plus user growth. The newer entrants (Speak, Praktika and other AI-tutor apps) differentiate on **speaking with realtime AI voice**. The input-heavy niche (LingQ), the community-correction niche (Busuu), the SRS niche (Anki with FSRS, Clozemaster) and the audio-only niche (Pimsleur) persist.

Outcome evidence is thin and mostly vendor-funded. Gamified apps show ACTFL-measurable **reading** gains in tens of hours, with **listening and especially speaking lagging**. The only causal evidence from deployed features concerns *engagement*: the SRS scheduler, leagues and notifications, mostly from Duolingo's own A/B tests. Learning outcomes from those features have not been shown causally.

### Cited Findings

**Duolingo: scale and strategy, 2025–2026**
- DAUs were **56.5M in Q1 2026 (+21% YoY) and 58.7M in Q2 2026 (+23% YoY)**. The 2026 strategy prioritises "teaching better" and user growth. [VENDOR, primary SEC filing] — [Duolingo Q2 2026 shareholder letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm); [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026029790/q1fy26duolingo3-31x26share.htm) [SIB: motivation_gamification.md]
- As of 31 Dec 2025, about **15M DAUs had a 365+ day streak, about 43M had a 7+ day streak, and about one-third of DAUs had a Friend Streak**. [VENDOR, 10-K] — [Duolingo FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm) [SIB: motivation_gamification.md]
- **Streak Revival (June 2026):** a one-time, opt-in event let learners restore their longest-ever streak by doing three lessons. **15.4M revived a streak, about 8M of whom had no active streak.** [VENDOR] — [Q2 2026 letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001562088/000162828026053299/q2fy26duolingo6-30x26share.htm) [SIB]
- DAU growth in 2024 was roughly 50%+ YoY, so the H1 2026 figures (+21–23%) are a marked deceleration. In its Q3 2025 results (Nov 2025) management said it would favour long-term user growth and "teaching better" over near-term monetization, and the share price fell sharply. [MK, medium confidence; the 2024 growth rate and the Q3 2025 framing are not verified this session] — [Duolingo IR](https://investors.duolingo.com) (homepage, not opened)

**Duolingo: product changes, chronological (older items marked)**
- **The Path redesign (late 2022, OLDER):** the branching skill "tree" was replaced by a single linear path of units. Learners lost free choice of skills but gained clearer next steps. Long-time users complained about the loss of choice and of test-outs at the time. [MK, high confidence on the change; complaint characterisation is model knowledge] — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com) (homepage, not opened)
- **Forums and sentence discussions closed (around 2022, OLDER).** Peer explanations of individual sentences, a valued community feature, were removed. [MK, medium confidence on timing]
- **The volunteer course-contributor programme ended in March 2021 (OLDER)**, after 1,000+ volunteers had built dozens of courses since 2013 (Navajo, Hawaiian, Welsh, Irish). It was replaced by paid content creation. [VENDOR/official] — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/ending-honoring-our-volunteer-contributor-program-2/) [SIB: heritage_minority_kurdish.md]
- **Welsh course development was paused in Oct/Nov 2023** (2M+ starters, 659k active), with resources moved to high-demand courses. The Welsh Government protested. [PRESS/official] — [Senedd research brief](https://business.senedd.wales/documents/s150940/Research%20brief.html?CT=2); [ITV News](https://www.itv.com/news/wales/2023-10-24/language-learning-app-to-pause-updates-on-its-welsh-course-from-next-month) [SIB]. Whether it resumed by 2026 was not verified.
- **Math and Music** were added to the main app in Oct 2023 (Math began as a standalone app in 2022). **Chess** was added in 2025, first as an iOS beta, later with player-vs-player matches. Duolingo is positioning itself as a general "learn anything daily" app. [MK, high confidence for Math and Music, medium for Chess timing]
- **Duolingo Max (March 2023, GPT-4):** Explain My Answer and Roleplay at launch, later **Video Call with Lily** (announced around Sept 2024) and "Adventures". [MK for dates; SIB lists the bundle] — [StoryLearning review](https://storylearning.com/duolingo-max-review); [Beginners in AI](https://beginnersinai.org/duolingo-max-explained/) [SIB: ai_llm_tutoring.md, secondary]
- **Explain My Answer has reportedly been free for all learners since 1 Jan 2026** in most courses teaching Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Portuguese, Italian and Korean. **SUPERSEDED:** it was Max-only at its 2023 launch. — [CopyCat Café](https://copycatcafe.com/blog/duolingo-max) [SIB: ai_llm_tutoring.md; secondary snippet, not confirmed on a Duolingo page]
- **Roleplay and Video Call** give feedback on the "accuracy and complexity" of what the learner said, after the conversation rather than during it. — [CopyCat Café](https://copycatcafe.com/blog/duolingo-max) [SIB, snippet]
- **Video Call efficacy:** Duolingo's Efficacy Research Lab reports that Video Call improves speaking and reduces speaking anxiety. **No effect sizes were retrieved.** [VENDOR, via aggregator] — [FeedBagel](https://feedbagel.com/post/duolingo-study-shows-video-calls-improve-language-learners-speaking-skills) [SIB]
- **Hearts → energy (2025).** Under the classic free tier, a learner had 5 hearts and lost one per mistake, then had to wait, practise to earn hearts back, pay gems or subscribe (Super gives unlimited hearts). During 2025 Duolingo tested and widely rolled out an **"energy"** meter instead. Each exercise consumes energy whether the answer is right or wrong (reportedly 25 max, 1 per exercise). Energy recharges over time and can be topped up; Super and Max have unlimited energy. The **"hearts" model is SUPERSEDED** for most users, though rollout may vary by platform or market. [MK, medium confidence on mechanics and rollout extent; not verified this session]
- **"AI-first" memo (28 April 2025):** CEO Luis von Ahn told staff Duolingo would "gradually stop using contractors to do work AI can handle". AI use would factor into hiring and performance reviews, and headcount would be added only where work could not be automated. This followed **cuts to about 10% of contractors in Jan 2024** that were attributed partly to AI. The backlash on social media was strong; Duolingo briefly paused or cleared its TikTok and Instagram posting in May 2025. Von Ahn later said the memo was poorly communicated and that no full-time employees had been laid off. [MK, high confidence on the memo and backlash; medium on details] — [Duolingo IR](https://investors.duolingo.com) (homepage, not opened)
- **148 new AI-built courses (30 April 2025):** Duolingo announced 148 courses created with generative AI in under a year, against about 12 years to build its first 100. This roughly doubled its course count. They mainly bring the 7 most popular non-English languages to Duolingo's ~28 UI languages, at beginner (about A1–A2) depth. Learner reception was mixed: users praised the breadth but reported thinness and errors. [MK, high confidence on the announcement; reception is model knowledge with no systematic data]
- **Matched weekly leagues** (cohorts of about 30, matched on prior-week engagement) raised total learning time by **17%** and **tripled** "highly engaged" learners. Leaderboards were the retention team's "first breakthrough". [VENDOR, ex-CPO account, covers about 2018–2022, OLDER] — [Lenny's Newsletter (Mazal)](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-duolingo-reignited-user-growth) [SIB: motivation_gamification.md]
- **Streak experiments:** 600+ over about 4 years. Separating the streak from the daily goal (one lesson keeps the streak) gave **+40%** learners on 7+ day streaks. Two equipped streak freezes gave **+0.38% DAU**. The Streak Wager gave **D7 +14%**. [VENDOR, via secondary summaries] — [Recall summary of Lenny's Podcast](https://www.getrecall.ai/summary/lennys-podcast/behind-the-product-duolingo-streaks-or-jackson-shuttleworth-group-pm-retention-team); [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak) [SIB]
- **Spaced-repetition scheduler (Half-Life Regression, 2016, OLDER):** its prediction error was about half of Leitner's, trained on 13M learning traces. In an A/B test it gave **+9.5% daily retention for practice, +1.7% for lessons and +12% overall activity**. These are engagement outcomes, not proficiency outcomes. [VENDOR, peer-reviewed, ACL] — [Settles & Meeder 2016](https://research.duolingo.com/papers/settles.acl16.pdf) [SIB: memory_retention.md]
- **Notification bandit:** +0.5% DAU and +2% new-user retention. [RCT/ML deployment, VENDOR] — [Yancey & Settles, KDD 2020](https://research.duolingo.com/papers/yancey.kdd20.pdf) [SIB]

**Duolingo: learning-outcome studies**
- **Jiang et al. 2021 (Foreign Language Annals):** adults who completed beginning-level Spanish or French scored **comparably to university students after 4 semesters on ACTFL reading and listening**, in roughly half the time. **Listening was significantly weaker than reading.** There was no randomised control. [OBS, VENDOR-authored] — [Wiley](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/flan.12600) [SIB: sla_theory.md]
- **Smith, Jiang & Peters 2024 (LLT 28(1)):** n = 48, 3 months, about **27 h**. Learners reached **ACTFL Intermediate Low in reading and Novice High in listening**; gains were linked to session completion and accuracy. [OBS, VENDOR-authored] — [LLT](https://www.lltjournal.org/item-detail/1152/) [SIB]
- **Kittredge et al. 2024 speaking report:** n = 140 beginners. **Only 66% of Spanish and 53% of French learners reached A2 speaking or higher.** [VENDOR] — [Duolingo whitepaper](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_language_conversation_2024.pdf) [SIB, snippet]
- **SSLA 2026, three-arm comparison** (Duolingo vs classroom vs classroom + Duolingo, beginner French): all improved with **similar magnitudes**. Only pragmatics (*tu* vs *vous*) favoured classroom + Duolingo. [QE; authors and funding not retrieved] — [Cambridge/SSLA](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/studies-in-second-language-acquisition/article/comparing-the-effectiveness-of-duolingo-classroom-instruction-and-classroom-duolingo-instruction-conditions-on-beginnerlevel-french-language-development/68C0E7E296669798089C84CDC7F3BB9E) [SIB]
- **Loewen et al. 2019:** 9 learners of Turkish. Gains were stronger in written than oral skills, and time on app correlated with gains. [OBS, independent, tiny n] — [ERIC](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1226279) [SIB]
- **Attrition:** 601 Duolingo beginners were followed for 6 months. **L2 grit and age** best predicted dropout. [OBS] — [ScienceDirect 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1041608025000809) [SIB]

**Babbel**
- **Loewen et al. 2020 (Babbel Spanish, FLA):** about **69% improved noticeably on an oral proficiency interview** despite limited in-app speaking, and **hours of use predicted gains on all tests**. [OBS, Babbel-commissioned] — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/341904580_The_effectiveness_of_app-based_language_instruction_for_developing_receptive_linguistic_knowledge_and_oral_communicative_ability); [MSU Today](https://msutoday.msu.edu/news/2020/04/how-effective-are-language-learning-apps) [SIB]
- Product model: subscription-first (essentially no free tier beyond a first lesson), dialogue-based lessons with explicit grammar notes in the learner's L1, a review manager (SRS), podcasts and games, and **Babbel Live** (paid small-group live classes with human teachers, since about 2020–21). AI conversation and speaking practice were added around 2024–2025; feature names and details are unverified. [MK] — [Babbel](https://www.babbel.com) (homepage, not opened)
- **SUPERSEDED/OLDER:** Babbel- and Busuu-commissioned "X hours = one college semester" studies (Vesselinov & Grego, 2012–2016) used non-standard instruments. They are superseded as evidence by the ACTFL/OPI-based studies above. [MK on the older studies; SIB: sla_theory.md treats them as superseded]

**Busuu**
- Its distinctive feature is **community correction**: learners submit written or spoken answers and native speakers of that language, who are also users, correct them. Courses are CEFR-aligned. Busuu was acquired by **Chegg (2021, about $436M)**. It is freemium, with Premium and Premium Plus tiers. [MK, high confidence on the feature and acquisition; medium on price] — [Busuu](https://www.busuu.com) (homepage, not opened)
- No independent outcome study of community correction was retrieved. Corrective-feedback meta-analyses in general support feedback on output; see sla_theory.md and ai_llm_tutoring.md. [Gap]

**Memrise**
- Memrise moved from user-generated "community courses" (largely removed from the app around 2022–23, to user complaints) to official courses built around **videos of native speakers** ("Learn with Locals"). It added an AI chat partner (**MemBot**, 2023). [MK, medium confidence] — [Memrise](https://www.memrise.com) (homepage, not opened)

**Speak (AI-native, speaking-first)**
- Speak runs on **OpenAI's Realtime API** plus its own curriculum and feedback systems. It claims **15M+ downloads and a $1B valuation (late 2024)**. [VENDOR via secondary] — [CO/AI profile](https://getcoai.com/tool-detail/speak/164726/) [SIB: ai_llm_tutoring.md]. Model knowledge adds a $78M Series C (Dec 2024) and strongest traction in Korea and Japan. [MK]

**Praktika (AI avatars)**
- **$35.5M Series A (May 2024)**; claims **1.2M MAU** in 100 countries and **about $20M trailing-12-month revenue**. [VENDOR via press] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/praktika-raises-35-5m-to-use-ai-avatars-to-make-learning-languages-feel-more-natural) [SIB]
- **Voice quality drove engagement:** in A/B tests against other text-to-speech engines, "metrics dropped noticeably". A viral "upgraded voices" campaign pushed it into the global top 10 language apps. [VENDOR] — [ElevenLabs case study](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/praktika-scales-immersive-language-learning-with-elevenlabs-tts) [SIB]
- **Independent evidence on AI speaking apps** is mostly small local studies (e.g. ELSA Speak) without robust designs. [OBS/QE] — [IAIN Palopo journal](https://ejournal.iainpalopo.ac.id/index.php/ideas/article/download/6573/4196/24217) [SIB]
- **The broader evidence base for dialogue-based CALL and chatbots in L2 speaking** is positive: **g = 0.61 (95% CI 0.34–0.89)**, from short studies, mostly English as a foreign language and prone to novelty effects. [MA] — [ReCALL](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/recall/article/dialoguebased-computerassisted-language-learning-systems-for-second-language-speaking-development-a-threelevel-metaanalysis/31847710516602398819C5E594038E7B) [SIB: ai_llm_tutoring.md]

**LingQ (input-first)**
- Learners read and listen to graded or imported content (articles, YouTube and podcast transcripts) and tap unknown words to save them as "LingQs" with dictionary glosses. Saved words get SRS review, and the app tracks a known-words count. "Mini-stories" with audio cover beginners. The free tier caps saved words; Premium is unlimited. [MK, high confidence on the design] — [LingQ](https://www.lingq.com) (homepage, not opened)
- The underlying evidence for reading-while-listening, glosses and extensive reading is meta-analytic; see sla_theory.md (for example, L1 glosses beat L2 glosses; captions g = 0.56). [SIB: sla_theory.md, multimedia_ux.md]

**Drops, Mondly, Clozemaster, Pimsleur, Anki**
- **Drops:** visual vocabulary in short swipe-based sessions. The free tier is **time-limited** (about 5 minutes every 10 hours, a different kind of limiter from hearts or energy). It was acquired by Kahoot! in 2020. [MK, medium] — [Drops](https://languagedrops.com) (homepage, not opened)
- **Mondly:** about 40 languages, chatbot and AR/VR lessons; acquired by Pearson in 2022. [MK, medium-high] — [Mondly](https://www.mondly.com) (homepage, not opened)
- **Clozemaster:** fill-in-the-blank on large sentence corpora (Tatoeba-derived), ordered by word frequency. It targets intermediate learners who have "finished Duolingo". [MK, high on the design] — [Clozemaster](https://www.clozemaster.com) (homepage, not opened)
- **Pimsleur:** audio-only 30-minute lessons built on prompted recall at expanding intervals ("graduated interval recall", 1960s). It is subscription-based (Simon & Schuster) and has added app features such as flashcards and speaking challenges. [MK, high on the method; medium on recent features] — [Pimsleur](https://www.pimsleur.com) (homepage, not opened)
- **Anki and FSRS:** FSRS was integrated into **Anki 23.10 (2023)** as an optional scheduler. **FSRS-6** (21 parameters, a per-user forgetting-curve shape) has log loss **0.3460** on the open benchmark, against **0.4694 for Duolingo's HLR**; SM-2 is far worse. FSRS-6 is now shipped by other language apps as well. [Benchmark, prediction rather than learning outcomes] — [srs-benchmark](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/srs-benchmark); [Anki 23.10 changes](https://changes.ankiweb.net/changes/23.10.html); [awesome-fsrs](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/awesome-fsrs) [SIB: memory_retention.md]
- Anki's model: free on desktop and Android, with a **paid one-time iOS app** funding development. [MK, high] — [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) (homepage, not opened)

### Inferences
- **Feature-by-feature view.** "Evidence" means outcome evidence for the feature itself, not engagement.

| Feature | Who has it (2026) | Best evidence for the feature | Status for a new app |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linear course path / unit map | Duolingo, Busuu, Babbel, most | None as a feature; reading gains for whole courses (OBS, VENDOR) | Table stakes |
| XP, streaks, freezes | Duolingo, nearly all | Engagement only (VENDOR A/B); streak misuse documented (QUAL) | Table stakes (forgiving version) |
| Matched weekly leagues | Duolingo, copied widely | +17% learning time (VENDOR); leaderboards can hurt low-ranked learners (QE) | Common, optional |
| SRS review of learned items | Duolingo (HLR/Birdbrain), Babbel, Anki (FSRS), Clozemaster, LingQ | Spacing MA (strong); HLR A/B +12% activity (VENDOR) | **Table stakes**; FSRS-6 is best in class |
| Explanations of mistakes in L1 | Duolingo (free since 2026, reportedly), Babbel | Corrective-feedback MAs (strong); no feature RCT | **Becoming table stakes** |
| AI role-play / voice call | Duolingo Max, Speak, Praktika, Memrise | Dialogue-CALL MA g = 0.61 (short, EFL); vendor claims | Premium differentiator; hard for Kurdish |
| Human community correction | Busuu (and formerly Duolingo forums) | No feature study | Differentiator; fits Hevalo's community |
| Input library with tap-to-gloss and save word | LingQ, Memrise (videos), Babbel podcasts | Glosses, reading-while-listening, extensive reading MAs | **Big gap in gamified apps**; differentiator |
| Audio-only / hands-free course | Pimsleur, Duolingo audio lessons (MK) | Retrieval and spacing logic; no feature RCT | Differentiator for commuters and parents |
| Pronunciation record and compare / scoring | uTalk, ELSA, Speak, Duolingo | Pronunciation-instruction MAs (d ≈ 0.8, OLDER); HVPT g = 0.71 | Common; scoring needs ASR (Kurdish is weak) |
| Live human classes / tutors | Babbel Live, Preply, italki | Classroom + app beats either alone on pragmatics (QE) | Monetisable add-on |
| Non-language subjects (Math, Music, Chess) | Duolingo | None relevant | Not relevant to Hevalo |

- The consistent pattern across studies is that **receptive skills (reading) improve fastest and speaking is the weakest outcome** (Jiang 2021; Smith 2024; Kittredge 2024; Loewen 2019). AI-native competitors are positioned exactly at this gap. That gap is the main 2026 differentiation axis for major languages, and the hardest one for Kurdish because speech AI is weak.
- Duolingo's 2025–2026 arc shows that **monetization and AI-scale content can cost trust and growth**: the AI-first backlash, the energy changes, decelerating DAU growth, then a public pivot to "teaching better". For a mission-driven minority-language product, trust is a core asset.

## 2. Children's language and early-learning apps: design patterns, monetization, safety

### Takeaway
The best-regarded children's apps (Duolingo ABC, Khan Academy Kids) are **free, ad-free and audio-first**: spoken instructions, narration, a character guide, short activities, and no leaderboards or open chat. Commercial apps (Lingokids, Studycat, Gus on the Go) use subscriptions or per-language purchases behind parental gates. Independent evidence says most store "educational" apps are low quality and that **80% of apps used by 3–5-year-olds contain manipulative design**. Regulation tightened in 2025–2026: amended COPPA in force from 22 April 2026, the EU DSA minors guidelines (streaks and push notifications off by default for minors), and the UK Children's Code. Efficacy claims from kids' language apps are vendor-only.

### Cited Findings
- **Duolingo ABC** (ages 3–8, English early literacy) "relies heavily on audio narration and animation". All instructions and feedback are spoken. Narration is composited from audio pieces, e.g. "The letter team" + [letter] + [letter] + "says" + [phoneme]. [VENDOR] — [Duolingo blog: building Duolingo ABC for Android](https://blog.duolingo.com/a-good-read-building-duolingo-abc-for-android/); [Duolingo ABC "How we teach"](https://abc.duolingo.com/how-we-teach) [SIB: children.md]
- Duolingo ABC is **free with no ads and no subscription**, and has no hearts or energy limiter. [MK, high confidence]
- **Khan Academy Kids** (ages 2–8): **completely free, no ads, no subscriptions**, from a nonprofit. A character guide leads children through reading, maths, social-emotional and early-literacy activities, with a library of books and read-aloud content. [MK, high confidence] — [Khan Academy Kids](https://learn.khanacademy.org/khan-academy-kids/) (homepage, not opened)
- **Lingokids** (ages 2–8): "playlearning" with games, videos, songs and puzzles. It cites constructivism, socio-cultural theory and gamification. It claims a UC Davis study found a "**14% improvement in vocabulary compared to peers**"; design, sample and independence are unknown. [VENDOR] — [Lingokids research](https://lingokids.com/research); [Lingokids blog](https://lingokids.com/blog/posts/efficacy-study-backs-lingokids-playlearning-methodology) [SIB]. Monetization is freemium with a "Plus" subscription behind a parental gate. [MK, medium]
- **Gus on the Go** (preschool vocabulary, an owl guide, many languages, a one-time purchase per language) and **Studycat** (ages about 3–8, game-based Spanish, English, Chinese and others; subscription) were not researched this session. Descriptions are [MK, low-medium confidence]. — [Gus on the Go](https://www.gusonthego.com); [Studycat](https://studycat.com) (homepages, not opened)
- **Average effect of educational apps (preschool to grade 3):** rigorous studies average **+0.31 SD**. Effects are larger for preschool, for researcher-developed outcomes, and for **constrained skills (letters, phonics)** than for comprehension. [MA] — [Kim et al. 2021, AERA Open](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1323865.pdf) [SIB: children.md]
- **Technology-enhanced storybooks:** small gains for comprehension (**g = 0.17**) and expressive vocabulary (**g = 0.20**). **Multimedia (animation, music, sound) helped; interactive hotspots, games and dictionaries distracted**, most of all for less-advantaged children. [MA, OLDER-2015, still the main synthesis] — [Takacs, Swart & Bus 2015](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.3102/0034654314566989) [SIB]
- **Most top "educational" apps score low** on the Four Pillars (active, engaged, meaningful, social). Free apps scored lower than paid ones on some criteria because of ads and distractions. [OBS, content analysis] — [Meyer et al. 2021, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35282402/) [SIB]
- **Manipulative design:** **80%** of apps used by children aged 3–5 contained it, and **65%** used it to prolong play. Examples: characters pleading with a countdown, lures for accumulating daily rewards, and ads that cannot be closed for 20 seconds. It was more common in apps used by lower-SES children. [OBS] — [Radesky et al. 2022, JAMA Netw Open](https://www.ovid.com/journals/janop/fulltext/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.17641~prevalence-and-characteristics-of-manipulative-design-in); [Michigan Medicine](https://www.michiganmedicine.org/health-lab/design-tricks-commonly-used-monetize-young-childrens-app-use) [SIB]
- **Rewards and intrinsic motivation:** engagement-contingent rewards undermined free-choice intrinsic motivation (**d = −0.40**). [MA, OLDER-1999, contested] — [SIB: children.md, Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999]
- **Regulation, 2025–2026:**
  - The **amended COPPA rule** took effect on 23 June 2025, with compliance due by **22 April 2026**. It requires separate parental consent for third-party disclosure, a written security programme and retention policy, and neutral age screens for mixed-audience services. [LAW] — [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-finalizes-changes-childrens-privacy-rule-limiting-companies-ability-monetize-kids-data) [SIB]
  - The **EU DSA Art. 28 guidelines** (14 July 2025) call for **streaks, autoplay and push notifications to be off by default for minors**, private accounts by default, and no stranger contact. [LAW, guidance] — [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-guidelines-protection-minors) [SIB]
  - The **UK ICO Children's Code** bars nudges that weaken privacy. [LAW] — [ICO nudge techniques](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-and-services/audits/data-protection-audit-framework/toolkits/age-appropriate-design/nudge-techniques/) [SIB]
  - **California SB 243** (in force 1 Jan 2026) requires AI-companion disclosure to minors and a break reminder every 3 hours. [LAW] — [Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/ai-regulatory-update-californias-sb-243-mandates-companion-ai-safety-and-accoun.html) [SIB]
  - The **US KIDS Act (H.R. 7757)** passed the House on 29 June 2026 and was **not law as of Oct 2026**. [LAW-in-progress] — [GovTrack](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/bills/119/hr7757) [SIB]
- **Apple Kids Category / Google Families:** no third-party analytics or ads (narrow exceptions apply), and a **parental gate before external links, purchases and personal-data requests**. [DOCS via secondary] — [GoodBarber explainer](https://www.goodbarber.com/help/apple-rejection-r100/apple-rejection-apps-for-kids-a365/) [SIB]
- **Children's ASR:** Whisper's word error rate is reportedly about **25% on child speech vs about 3% for adults**. Kurdish child speech would be worse. [OBS, secondary] — [Learning Agency](https://the-learning-agency.com/the-cutting-ed/article/how-speech-recognition-systems-struggle-with-childrens-voices/) [SIB]

### Inferences
- **Shared design patterns across the best kids' apps:**
  - audio narration of every instruction, so no reading is needed;
  - one friendly guide character;
  - 2–5-minute activities;
  - a collectible reward economy with no real-money lures;
  - a parent area behind a gate;
  - no open social features, leaderboards or chat;
  - offline play.
- None of the leading kids' apps uses hearts or energy limiters. Error-penalising limiters are an adult-app monetization device and conflict with child-wellbeing guidance.
- The **free, ad-free nonprofit model** (Khan Kids, Duolingo ABC as a loss leader) defines parent expectations for "trusted". A Kurdish kids' mode would be judged against that standard.
- Phonics and letter knowledge are where apps show their largest effects (Kim 2021). This maps directly onto an alphabet module for Hawar Latin and Sorani scripts.

### Gaps
- No independent peer-reviewed efficacy study of Duolingo ABC, Lingokids, Gus on the Go or Studycat was found by the sibling researcher; none was searchable here.
- 2026 pricing and features of Lingokids, Studycat and Gus on the Go were not verified, nor whether Gus on the Go is still maintained.
- No children's language app for Kurdish with independent reviews was found.

## 4. Monetization approaches that preserve learning (hearts, energy, time limits, subscriptions) and their pedagogical side effects

### Takeaway
Four freemium "limiters" dominate:
- **error-based** (Duolingo hearts, OLDER and now largely SUPERSEDED);
- **volume-based** (Duolingo energy, 2025);
- **time-based** (Drops);
- **feature or content paywalls** (Babbel's near-hard paywall, Busuu, LingQ word caps; Max for AI features).

Error-based limiters punish exactly the mistakes that learning requires. Volume and time limiters cap practice and therefore learning time, which is the strongest predictor of gains in app studies. AI features and human services (live classes, tutors, corrections) are the monetization layers least in conflict with learning. Kids' apps that parents trust are free or simple subscriptions with no lures.

### Cited Findings
- **Time on app predicts learning.** Hours of use predicted gains on all tests for Babbel. [OBS, vendor-commissioned] — [MSU Today](https://msutoday.msu.edu/news/2020/04/how-effective-are-language-learning-apps). Gains in Duolingo users were linked to session completion. [OBS, VENDOR-authored] — [Smith et al. 2024, LLT](https://www.lltjournal.org/item-detail/1152/) [both SIB: sla_theory.md]
- **Gamification misuse:** nine years of Duolingo forum posts plus 15 interviews show learners fixating on streaks and XP, taking shortcuts (e.g. replaying easy lessons) and being distracted from learning. [QUAL] — [Mogavi et al. 2022, arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.16175) [SIB: motivation_gamification.md]
- **Leaderboards:** in a 2024 quasi-experiment they did not raise optional practice and **lowered exam scores**. [QE] — [ScienceDirect 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1041608024001651) [SIB]
- **Children:** manipulative monetization design is common, with daily-reward lures and countdowns more frequent in apps used by lower-SES children. [OBS] — [Radesky et al. 2022](https://www.michiganmedicine.org/health-lab/design-tricks-commonly-used-monetize-young-childrens-app-use) [SIB]. Free kids' apps score lower on quality because of ads and distraction. [OBS] — [Meyer et al. 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35282402/) [SIB]
- **Hearts mechanics (OLDER, largely SUPERSEDED):** 5 hearts, minus 1 per mistake. Learners could refill by waiting, doing practice sessions, paying gems or subscribing; Super removed the limit. **Energy (2025):** a cost per exercise regardless of correctness, recharging over time, unlimited for subscribers. [MK, medium confidence on specifics]
- **AI paywall loosening:** Explain My Answer moved from Max-only (2023) to reportedly **free for all from Jan 2026**. Realtime conversation (Video Call) remains premium. — [CopyCat Café](https://copycatcafe.com/blog/duolingo-max) [SIB, secondary snippet]
- **AI cost structure:** a cached explanation per (exercise, wrong answer) pair turns per-request LLM cost into a one-off, reviewable cost. Realtime voice is per-minute. — [SIB: ai_llm_tutoring.md, inference]
- **Other models [MK]:**
  - Babbel: subscription-only, plus paid live classes.
  - Busuu: freemium, with community correction partly gated by tier.
  - LingQ: free tier caps saved words.
  - Drops: free tier caps minutes per 10 hours.
  - Anki: free, with a paid iOS app.
  - Pimsleur: subscription.
  - Khan Academy Kids: free (philanthropy).
  - Duolingo ABC: free.
  - Lingokids and Studycat: subscription behind a parental gate.
  - Gus on the Go: paid per language.

### Inferences
- **Hearts are pedagogically backwards.** They penalise errors, which discourages attempting harder items and productive guessing. Error-correction research (covered by other researchers) indicates that errors followed by feedback support learning. Hearts also push struggling learners, those who make most mistakes, out of the app soonest. Energy removes the error penalty but caps volume; a volume cap still reduces the strongest predictor of gains (time on task). The least harmful limiter caps *new content per day*, never review. The SRS literature recommends that anyway, to prevent review debt.
- **Monetization least in tension with learning:**
  - cosmetics (Hevalo's Zêr shop);
  - convenience (offline packs, ad-free);
  - expensive-to-serve features (AI conversation, human corrections, live classes);
  - institutional licences (schools, diaspora programmes);
  - family plans.
- Duolingo's 2025 path (energy, AI-first, then a 2026 pivot back to "teaching better") is a cautionary signal: aggressive monetization of the core learning loop cost goodwill at the market leader.

### Gaps
- No public data were retrieved on the **learning** effect, as opposed to revenue or DAU effects, of hearts vs energy. Duolingo has not published this, to my knowledge.
- No study comparing freemium limiter types on learning outcomes was found.
- Conversion rates and price points for 2026 were not verified.

## 5. What users complain about in reviews of leading apps

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints:
1. **Not enough real speaking or listening.** Apps teach recognition, not conversation. This is backed by outcome data: speaking and listening lag reading.
2. **Gamification over learning:** streak anxiety, XP farming, leagues, guilt-tripping notifications.
3. **Monetization friction:** hearts and energy, ads, paywalled explanations.
4. **AI content quality and "AI-first" distrust** (Duolingo 2025).
5. **Loss of community and user choice:** the forums, the tree-to-path change, Memrise community courses.

Systematic review-mining data (counts or percentages) were not retrieved this session.

### Cited Findings
- **Speaking and listening gap:**
  - Only 53–66% of Duolingo beginners reached A2 speaking. [VENDOR] — [Kittredge et al. 2024](https://duolingo-papers.s3.amazonaws.com/reports/Duolingo_whitepaper_language_conversation_2024.pdf)
  - Listening was weaker than reading. [OBS] — [Jiang et al. 2021](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/flan.12600)
  - Oral skills lag written skills. [OBS] — [Loewen et al. 2019](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1226279)
  - [all SIB: sla_theory.md]
- **Gamification over learning:**
  - Users fixate on streaks and XP, game leagues (including with paid hacking tools), and see rankings as unfair. [QUAL] — [Mogavi et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.16175) [SIB]
  - Parents describe children doing "performative learning" for the streak. [EXP, opinion] — [Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/guides/duolingo-streaks-and-anxiety-in-kids) [SIB]
  - Snapchat-streak research links streaks to problematic use in adolescents. [OBS] — [ScienceDirect 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2772503023000476) [SIB]
- **Attrition reasons:** reduced motivation, social isolation, anxiety, lack of success, poor self-regulation, lack of guidance, inauthentic content, poor instructional quality and technical issues. [QUAL/thesis] — [McGill thesis](https://escholarship.mcgill.ca/downloads/k643b581s?locale=en) [SIB]
- **AI and "AI-first" distrust:** the 2025 backlash over replacing contractors with AI, and complaints about thin or erroneous AI-generated courses. [MK, high confidence that the backlash occurred; no quantitative sentiment data]
- **Monetization friction:** complaints that energy lets free users do fewer exercises, that ad load is high, and that features were moved behind Max. [MK, medium; from widely discussed Reddit and app-store sentiment, not systematically measured]
- **Loss of community and choice:** removal of Duolingo forums and sentence discussions; the linear path replacing the tree (2022); Memrise removing community courses from its app. [MK, medium]
- **AI voice quality** affects engagement: Praktika's A/B tests. [VENDOR] — [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/praktika-scales-immersive-language-learning-with-elevenlabs-tts) [SIB]

### Inferences
- The complaints line up with the research gaps:
  - speaking and listening are under-supplied;
  - extrinsic mechanics can crowd out learning;
  - learners value human explanation and community, which Duolingo removed and partly re-sold as AI.
- For Hevalo, Kurdish-specific frustrations are likely to be:
  - **dialect mismatch** ("this isn't how my family says it");
  - **script mismatch**;
  - **missing native audio**;
  - **content that is too basic for heritage speakers**.

  This is inferred from heritage-learner research in heritage_minority_kurdish.md; no Kurdish app reviews were retrieved.

### Gaps
- No systematic, quantified analysis of 2025–2026 app-store reviews (e.g. the share of reviews mentioning speaking, ads or energy) was retrieved.
- No review data for Kurdish apps.

## Implications for Hevalo

Concrete design implications drawn from the landscape above. Each item carries the evidence grade of its main support. "Table stakes" means leading apps already do this and users will expect it.

**A. Close the table-stakes gaps against Duolingo and Babbel**

1. **A real SRS review loop (FSRS-6), fed by every graded answer** in lessons, alphabet drills and all games, with a daily "Review" entry on the course map.
   - Due reviews earn XP, streak credit and league points equal to new lessons.
   - Cap *new* items per day when review debt grows; never cap reviews.
   - Support: spacing MA; Duolingo HLR A/B +12% activity [VENDOR]; FSRS-6 best on the open benchmark.
2. **A free "Why was this wrong?" explanation** in the learner's UI language (nine languages).
   - The exercise engine decides correctness deterministically.
   - Explanations come from vetted grammar notes, are LLM-phrased only in high-resource UI languages, are cached per (item, wrong answer), and are native-reviewed for the most frequent errors.
   - Duolingo made this free in 2026 (secondary snippet), so it is table stakes and should not be paywalled.
3. **Native-speaker audio on every word and sentence**, for both Kurmancî and Soranî. Dialect and uTalk already market "every word recorded by a native speaker", so this is the Kurdish-market bar.
   - Prefer human recordings over TTS. Praktika's A/B evidence shows voice quality drives engagement [VENDOR], and Kurmanji TTS is unproven.
4. **Heritage placement and test-out by skill** (listening, reading, spelling) instead of forcing everyone through Duolingo's linear path from zero. Restore the "choice" users mourned when Duolingo moved from the tree to the path: let any unit be tested out of.
5. **Forgiving streaks:**
   - one activity of any kind keeps the streak;
   - freezes are earned by learning;
   - a repair window;
   - an annual opt-in "streak revival" event, which recovered 15.4M streaks at Duolingo in June 2026 [VENDOR]. Newroz (21 March) is the natural date.

**B. Differentiate where leaders are weak and Kurdish learners need most**

6. **Graded Kurdish reading and listening library (the LingQ model)** built from the community stories and poems feed:
   - editor-reviewed and level-tagged;
   - with audio;
   - tap any word for the 400k dictionary entry;
   - "save word" sends it into the FSRS queue, with its sentence as context.

   This attacks the most documented weakness of gamified apps (listening and input; Jiang 2021, Smith 2024), and the dictionary is an asset no Kurdish competitor has.
7. **Busuu-style community correction**, a "Rast bike / correct my Kurdish" request type in the feed. Learners submit short texts or voice clips; fluent users correct them for Zêr and reputation, with variety tags (Kurmancî or Soranî, region) so corrections respect dialect. Duolingo removed its forums and users missed them, so this is a community differentiator. There is no feature-level RCT; corrective-feedback MAs support it.
8. **Speaking without depending on weak Kurdish ASR:**
   - "listen, record, compare" and shadowing modes now;
   - minimal-pair listening games, slotted into the Wordle and rhyme families;
   - automated scoring only after testing Omnilingual ASR or Whisper fine-tunes on Hevalo learner audio (Sorani first).

   Speaking is where every leading app underperforms (A2 speaking only 53–66% at Duolingo [VENDOR]), and it is the axis AI-native apps compete on.
9. **Scenario role-play with post-conversation feedback** (the Duolingo, Speak and Praktika pattern), but **text-first and scenario-bounded**, with Kurdish output checked against Hawar or Sorani script validators and dictionary coverage.
   - Gate realtime voice behind a premium tier only once Kurdish speech quality is validated.
   - Do not ship an open "chat about anything" bot for Kurdish.
10. **An audio-only "Pimsleur-style" mode** (prompted recall at expanding intervals, hands-free) for commuting adults and for diaspora parents learning "Kurdish to say to your child". No major competitor offers this for Kurdish.
11. **Teacher and class mode:**
    - class codes;
    - assignable units;
    - progress reports;
    - live Kahoot-style games for Turkey's Kurmancî elective (59k pupils, almost no new teachers) and for diaspora and KRG online schools (about 3k pupils).

    Competitors do not serve this channel.
12. **Publish Kurdish can-do levels ("aligned to CEFR descriptors")** and the first outcome data for any Kurdish app: pre/post placement and delayed retention checks. This credibility asset is unavailable to competitors, who publish no outcome data.

**C. Children: build a separate kids mode to Khan Kids / Duolingo ABC standards**

13. **Audio-first kids mode for ages 4–8:**
    - narrated instructions;
    - one Kurdish guide character;
    - the alphabet and phonics module as its core (constrained skills show the largest app effects, Kim 2021 [MA]);
    - decodable mini-stories with narration and no in-story hotspots or mini-games (Takacs 2015 [MA]).
14. **No hearts or energy, no ads, no countdowns, no loot boxes, no "log in daily to collect" rewards** in kids mode.
    - Zêr and cosmetics are earned only through learning milestones.
    - A parental gate guards purchases, external links and the shop.
    - Support: Radesky 2022 [OBS]; Apple/Google kids rules; COPPA 2025.
15. **No public feed, DMs, open multiplayer or public leagues for under-13s.** Use family circles (grandparents invited by code), private game rooms with auto-generated Kurdish nicknames, and preset phrases and stickers instead of free text. **Streaks and push notifications are off by default for minors** (EU DSA guidelines).
16. **Kid-safe dictionary view:** filter the Wiktionary-derived lexicon for vulgar and sexual entries, and remove external links in kids' mode.
17. **Parent dashboard** showing what was learned (letters, words, stories), plus three phrases a week to use at home.
18. **Licensed comprehensible input:** pursue Zarok TV clips, *Hano* magazine stories and bilingual picture books for kids' mode.

**D. Monetization that does not tax learning**

19. **Do not adopt hearts.** They penalise errors and drive out struggling learners. **Avoid energy limits on review, the alphabet and kids' mode.** If a free-tier limiter is ever needed, cap *new units per day* (which the SRS literature recommends anyway), never review or retrieval practice.
20. **Monetize layers that cost money to serve or add convenience:**
    - "Hevalo Plus": offline packs, ad-free use, AI role-play and explanations beyond a daily free quota, audio-only course downloads;
    - human services: priority community corrections, live small-group classes with Kurdish teachers (Babbel Live analogue, or Preply-style tutor matching);
    - family plans;
    - school and diaspora-organisation licences;
    - Zêr cosmetics.
21. **Diaspora patronage:** "sponsor a learner in Rojhilat/Bakur" or a supporter tier. This fits a mission-driven minority-language product. It has no outcome evidence and is a strategic inference.

**E. Trust and AI-content policy (lessons from Duolingo's 2025 backlash)**

22. **Label content provenance:**
    - "recorded by native speaker" and "reviewed by Kurdish editor" badges;
    - any AI-drafted content (exercise variants, explanations) human-reviewed before release;
    - a public statement that Kurdish content is made by Kurdish people.
23. **Keep a paid core editorial team plus a moderated community contribution pipeline.** Duolingo's volunteer model ended in 2021 and Welsh was paused in 2023: pure-volunteer or platform-dependent models are fragile.
24. **Dialect and script respect as a first-class feature:**
    - every item tagged by variety and script;
    - one-tap transliteration (Hawar Latin ↔ Kurdish Arabic) using existing open transliterators;
    - a "how does your family say it?" variant-submission flow.

    Dialect mismatch is the most likely Kurdish-specific complaint.

**F. Measure learning, not just engagement**

25. **Judge changes on learning outcomes.** Every A/B test on streaks, leagues, limiters or AI features should be judged on delayed retention (7–30-day spot checks inside reviews) and placement-score gains, segmented by learner type: heritage diaspora, in-region schooled in another language, non-Kurd, child. Most of the evidence above is engagement data or vendor-funded, and none of it is Kurdish.
