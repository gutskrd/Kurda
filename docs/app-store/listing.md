# The App Store listing

What goes into App Store Connect for Hevalo 1.0.0, in each language the store
offers that the app speaks. Kurdish is not one of the store's listing
languages, so a Kurdish speaker sees the English listing or the one in their
phone's language; the Kurdish words in it are kept in Hawar letters.

Everything here describes the app as it is in the 1.0.0 build. A sentence that
stops being true (a Soranî course arrives, something is sold) changes here in
the same change that makes it so. `node scripts/check-listing.mjs` checks every
field against the store's limits.

The screenshots are made from the app's own screens at 1320 × 2868, the
6.9-inch iPhone size the store scales down for every smaller iPhone; they need
no iPad set, because the app is not offered on iPad (`supportsTablet: false`).
